# Review input cutover

These are the exact previous working bytes of the three legacy correction inputs.
Issue 51 supersedes them with `data/reference/review/decisions.jsonl` and the manual
institution registry. The implementing commit records the cutover.

Three education assertions and four sourced school relationships were migrated.
The remaining 89 school aliases are research decisions with exact original values
and source-file hashes. A matching ACARA target alone is not relationship evidence.
Original absent reviewers and evidence retrieval dates remain absent; migration
attestations identify the importer and their actual date separately.

Do not edit these files or use them as active corrections. They support the
independent migration oracle and historical reproduction of earlier outputs.
